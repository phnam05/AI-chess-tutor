**How to read this.** "Planted" is what the bot *tried* to do, not what its move really was: a crude "quiet" move can still lose material to a pin or fork, and skipping a recapture can let the opponent take more. The engine judges each move as it really is, so the last column shows how often the plan and the engine agree — and "other kinds flagged" can be a real weakness the bot showed, not a false alarm. Check the per-trial counts below before reading a miss as a failure of the learner model.
