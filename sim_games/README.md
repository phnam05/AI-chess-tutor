**Not real players.** Skill Level 0 is the bottom of Stockfish's own Elo scale (labelled 1320; there is no "1000" setting, and that scale isn't a human rating). Its mistakes come from a shallow search plus random noise, not from how people think. Use these games to see the panel work, not as evidence about real players.

Players A and B are the same bot, so any difference between their panels is luck.
To see a game in the app: open `game_NN.pgn`, copy all of it, paste it under "Review a full game (PGN)", and pick Player A's colour under "You played".
